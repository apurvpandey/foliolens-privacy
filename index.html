FolioLens Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · Developer: Apurv Pandey · Contact: mailapurvpandey@gmail.com

Summary

FolioLens keeps your portfolio on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting service in the app.

Data stored on your device only

The transactions you enter, your watchlist, compare list, alert rules, settings and downloaded NAV history are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer cannot see them. App backup is turned off. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can export your transactions as a CSV file to a place you choose.

Data sent over the internet

To show fund names and NAVs, the app contacts the public MFAPI service (api.mfapi.in) over HTTPS. It sends:

  • the scheme codes of funds the app needs data for, and
  • the text you type in the search box.

It never sends your transactions, units, amounts, gains or any personal details. Like any web service, MFAPI can see your IP address and the time of each request. The developer does not operate MFAPI and does not receive this data. Please see the MFAPI service for how it handles requests.

Permissions

  • Internet: to fetch NAV data.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13 and later): only if you turn on NAV alerts. No other use.
  • Biometrics or screen lock (optional): if you turn on App lock, the Android system shows its own prompt. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN, only the yes or no result.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

FolioLens is an educational tool. It gives no investment, tax or legal advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks.
